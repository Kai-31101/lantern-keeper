Privacy Policy

Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD · Updated 19 November 2026

This policy covers the online-enabled Android game Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD, package com.lanternkeeper.game. It supports local play, guest accounts, optional Google sign-in, cloud saves and Top Players for approved testers. Older offline-only versions keep progress on the device.

Accounts and cloud saves

When connected, Firebase Authentication can create an anonymous guest account with a unique user ID. Connected guests can synchronize progress to the cloud: Guest does not mean device-only storage. Optional Google sign-in links or restores progress. Google/Firebase process your account identifier, email and verification status, and may provide basic profile information such as your name and photo. The game does not request your Google password.

Cloud saves contain progress and settings, including unlocked content, gold, gems, equipment, upgrades and records, with a revision, progress summary and update time. They are stored under your Firebase user ID. Conflicting local/cloud saves require your choice; they are not merged automatically. You can continue locally if the network is unavailable.

Top Players

Approved Google testers can use Top Players. Private records include tester eligibility, run tickets, mode, chapter or Daily challenge, outcome, wave, elapsed game time, kills, client version, timestamps and rate-limit data. These support ranking, validation and abuse prevention.

Eligible players see generated pseudonyms, scores and ranks. Pseudonyms do not derive from Google names or emails. Leaderboard responses do not expose your email, Google name, photo, private user ID or save. Results with a valid ticket may be queued locally and retried for the same account; tickets are valid for up to three hours.

Optional diagnostics

Analytics and crash-report collection are off by default. You can change Diagnostics in Settings → Account & cloud save. When enabled, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process usage events, crash information, app/device information and installation or device identifiers to help improve the game. Game events include run start/end, level-up choices, chapter unlocks and tutorial steps. Turning Diagnostics off does not erase reports already sent.

Local data and service providers

Your device stores progress, settings, language choice, account preferences, pending results and recovery copies for account changes or save conflicts. Android backup may also handle app data according to your device settings. Clearing local storage does not delete cloud data.

Google/Firebase provide authentication, storage, backend processing and optional diagnostics. Network services process connection information such as IP addresses and SDK/app information for operation and security. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information. Support emails and details you send are used to respond to your request.

Advertising, purchases and permissions

Advertising and purchase features are disabled in this release. Related SDK libraries are included, but the game does not enable ad requests or purchase checkout. We do not ask for payment-card information. Internet supports online features; vibration provides optional feedback. The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location access.

Deletion and retention

Use Settings → Account & cloud save → Delete account, or the account deletion instructions. Completed deletion removes your Firebase game account, cloud save, ranked runs, leaderboard profile/entries, rate-limit record and tester entitlement. Local progress is kept until deletion completes; failed steps offer Retry deletion or Finish deletion.

Minimal private deletion-suppression metadata (user ID, time and cleanup state) is retained to stop delayed requests recreating deleted data. It contains no name, email, score or save. Account-owned recovery copies are removed; other accounts' copies remain. Older migration backups are removed only if you select that option. Clearing app storage removes remaining local copies. Account deletion does not automatically erase optional Analytics/Crashlytics reports already sent or support correspondence; contact support about those records.

Account and gameplay records are retained to provide these features until deleted. A ticket's three-hour validity is not an automatic deletion deadline for its stored record. Provider operational records and optional diagnostics follow the relevant service's retention controls.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact support so we can investigate and help with deletion.

Contact and changes

Questions or data requests: lanternkeeper.support@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We update this policy when data practices change.
