Privacy Policy

Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD · Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD (package com.lanternkeeper.game, "the game") handles. In short: the current version of the game does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases in its current version. It does not send any data to us or to third parties.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked chapters, gold, gems, gear, upgrades and settings such as volume) is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot access it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: short haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet: included by the app framework. The current version does not use it; all game content is bundled in the app.

Third-party services

The current version contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Google Play may collect information when you download the app, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update adds features that handle personal data (for example ads, purchases or cloud saves), we will update this policy before releasing that update and change the effective date above. The Data safety section on Google Play will be updated at the same time.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or to request deletion of any data: lanternkeeper.support@gmail.com
