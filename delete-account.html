Delete your Lantern Keeper account and data

For Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD, package com.lanternkeeper.game. These instructions cover online-enabled guest and Google-linked game accounts.

Request deletion without the app

Email lanternkeeper.support@gmail.com with the subject “Lantern Keeper account deletion”. For a Google-linked account, send the request from the linked email. For a guest account, include its player UID if you already have it. Never send passwords, payment-card details or sign-in codes.

We need to verify account ownership. If a guest account cannot be identified or verified, support will explain what information is needed. This route is available without the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Connect to the internet and open Settings → Account & cloud save.
  2. Select Delete account and confirm permanent removal of your game account, cloud save and Top Players data.
  3. If offered, choose whether to delete older migration backups on this device.
  4. If a step fails, use Retry deletion. If Google requires recent authentication, use Finish deletion and sign in to the same Google account. Local progress remains until the process completes.

What is removed

Completed deletion removes the Firebase game account, its cloud save, ranked runs, leaderboard profile/entries, rate-limit record and tester entitlement. It then removes active local progress, pending results and account-owned recovery copies. It does not delete your Google account itself. Other accounts' local copies remain; older migration backups remain unless you select their deletion.

What may remain

Minimal private deletion-suppression metadata (user ID, time and cleanup state) remains to prevent delayed requests recreating data. It contains no name, email, scores or save. Previously sent optional diagnostic reports, support correspondence and provider operational records are not automatically erased by the in-app flow. Contact support about these records.

Clearing app storage removes remaining local data. Uninstalling or clearing storage alone does not remove cloud data. Android backups are controlled separately by device settings. Ads and purchase checkout are disabled in this release.

Older offline-only versions do not create a cloud account; clearing their local data removes their progress.
