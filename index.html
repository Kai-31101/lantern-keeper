Lantern Keeper: Stickman TD

Tower defense roguelite for Android

Guard the steps of an ancient temple as waves of monsters march in. Your stickman hero fires on their own; every level-up you choose one of three cards: weapons, skills, passives, evolutions and fusions. No two runs play the same.

  • 3 heroes, 8 weapons that evolve, 6 active skills and 4 skill fusions
  • 5 chapters with bosses, plus Endless mode and a Daily Challenge
  • Gear, pets and permanent upgrades between runs
  • Plays fully offline
